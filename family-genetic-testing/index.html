One Test, Many Patients: Family-Level Genetic Testing at Scale

Raj Hansini, Kanix Wang, Dan Adelman

University of Chicago · In progress

Full write-up coming soon.

Overview

  • Clinical testing decisions are made one patient at a time, yet a genetic result also reveals the risk of every blood relative, so the true value of a test belongs to the family.
  • Planning tests across the whole family is optimal but becomes computationally intractable beyond about five members, which keeps family-level planning out of clinical practice.
  • We are developing a graph neural network that learns these family-level decisions, scaling them to real pedigrees and adapting to new testing costs and disease risks without retraining.
